Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Who this covers

This policy covers "60secnews uploader", a private tool used only by the owner of the 60 Sec News India channel to publish its own daily news videos. The tool is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data the tool uses

  • Google / YouTube: the tool requests one permission, youtube.upload, to upload videos to the owner's own channel. It does not read, collect or store anyone else's data, and does not access viewer information.
  • Login credentials: the access token is stored privately by the owner and is used only to upload the channel's own videos.
  • News content: headlines are read from public RSS feeds of news outlets. No personal data is collected from them.

What we don't do

We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties, use data for advertising, or use cookies or analytics on this website.

Google API Services

This tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

For questions, contact the channel owner through the YouTube channel's About page.
